How is your estimated date calculated?

First, the calculator counts all applicants who joined before your date and are still waiting for submission.

Because the source data is voluntarily self-reported, some people who are actually in the embassy queue may never enter their information into the tracker.

You can therefore choose a safety buffer. A larger buffer produces a more conservative estimate. The recommended default is 50 people.

Important disclaimer

This is an unofficial community forecasting tool. It is not affiliated with the German Embassy, the German Government, the Federal Foreign Office, or any official visa service provider.

The underlying source is self-reported community data. Entries may be missing, delayed, edited, inaccurate, or incomplete.

The calculated date is only an estimate based on the currently observed queue and processing speed. It is not an appointment guarantee.
