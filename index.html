How your estimate is calculated

The calculator first counts applicants who joined before you and are still waiting. Because this is a self-reported tracker and many real applicants may never enter their information, an additional 50-person safety buffer is added.

Your estimated queue is then divided by the observed processing speed to estimate how many business days remain. Saturdays and Sundays are excluded only when converting the remaining waiting time into the final expected submission date.

Your position could vary within your joining day

We know how many unresolved applicants joined on your date, but we do not know whether you were near the beginning, middle, or end of that day's queue. Therefore, the calculator shows three possible scenarios.
