How is your estimated date calculated?

First, the calculator counts all applicants who joined before your date and still have a blank submission field.

Because this tracker is self-reported, there are probably real applicants in the embassy queue who never entered their data. To account for this uncertainty, the calculator automatically adds a 50-person safety buffer to your estimated queue.

The estimated number of people ahead of you is then divided by the observed processing speed to estimate how many working days remain.

Important disclaimer

This is an unofficial community forecasting tool. It is not affiliated with the German Embassy, the German Government, the Federal Foreign Office, or any official visa service provider.

The underlying source is self-reported community data. Entries may be missing, delayed, incorrect, edited later, or incomplete.

The calculated date is only an estimate based on the currently observed queue and processing speed. It is not an appointment guarantee and should not be treated as an official visa timeline.
